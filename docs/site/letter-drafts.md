# Landing page letter drafts

Two drafts of the maker's letter for the landing page, kept for when the site is built. The page's first button goes to the live demo; a small download link sits under it.

## Draft 1: The math

Type to your AI for thirty minutes and it can build two days of work. Talk to it for thirty minutes and it can build two weeks.

I learned that the slow way. I talk to my agents now, a lot of them, all at once. A dozen Claude sessions on three screens, each one waiting to hear what I want.

And my night became: find the right window, click it, talk. Find, click, talk.

So I got rid of the find and the click. I look at a session. I pinch. I talk. Thumbs-up. Next.

If you've already stopped typing to your agents, you're one click from leaning back.

## Draft 2: One night

One night I had eleven Claude sessions running and I realised I hadn't typed in hours. I'd just been talking.

Thirty minutes of my voice had turned into what used to take me two weeks. The only thing slowing me down was me, finding the window, clicking it, sitting up straight.

So I made my Mac watch where I look. Now I lean back, look at a session, pinch twice, say what I mean, and give it a thumbs-up.

If you run your agents in parallel and talk to them, you already know why I built this.
