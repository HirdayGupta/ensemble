Control Claude
without touching a key.

Look at a Claude Code session, pinch twice to dictate, hold a thumbs-up to send. A webcam and your hands, running entirely on your Mac.

Download for macOS View on GitHub

Free and open source (MIT). macOS 14.2 or later, tested on Apple Silicon. Needs Claude desktop.

Four gestures. That is all.

A status capsule at the top of the screen shows what the app sees and what it is about to do, so nothing happens by surprise.

Look to focus

Face a Claude session and it lights up. Your nose is the pointer; every gesture goes to the session you face.

Pinch twice to dictate

Touch thumb to index finger twice, quickly, to start Claude's dictation. Do it again to stop. Your hand can leave the frame in between.

Hold a thumbs-up to send

Hold it for about a second and the dictated draft is sent. It works only within 30 seconds of dictation ending, and only with a non-empty draft.

Pinch and drag to scroll

Pinch, then move your hand up or down. The session scrolls at your system's own scroll direction and speed.

Your camera never leaves your Mac.

There is no account, no server and no analytics. The app makes no network connections.

On-device only

Face and hand tracking run locally. The face model ships inside the app, so nothing is downloaded or uploaded.

Frames are never stored

Each frame is analysed in memory and discarded. The log records events and reasons, never images.

The microphone is never left on

A record is written before dictation starts, a dead-man check watches while it runs, and a fallback mutes the input if a stop cannot be confirmed.

Questions

Short answers. The README has the details.

What permissions does it ask for?

Camera, to read your head direction and hand pose. Accessibility, to find Claude's session panes and press its dictation, Send and scroll controls. Notifications are optional. It does not ask for the Microphone: Claude does the recording, and the app only watches which apps are recording.

Which Claude apps does it work with?

Claude desktop for Mac, in its Code sessions. It does not work with the browser version or with the terminal. It only acts when Claude is the app under your gaze; with any other app in front, gestures do nothing.

It reads Claude's interface through Accessibility, so a Claude update that renames its controls can break it until the app is updated.

Does it record me?

No. Video is analysed on your Mac and is never written to disk or sent anywhere. Dictation itself is Claude's own feature; this app only presses its button.

Can it send something by accident?

Sending needs a deliberate thumbs-up hold, a non-empty draft, dictation that ended in the last 30 seconds, and no keyboard or mouse use since. Typing, clicking, a second face in view or a covered camera all cancel it. You can also turn gesture send off in Settings.

Which Macs does it support?

macOS 14.2 or later with a webcam. It is built and tested on Apple Silicon; Intel has not been tried.

Is it affiliated with Anthropic?

No. It is an independent open-source project.
